# Landing product evidence

Reusable synthetic assets and captions for issue #22 and the documentation handoff to #23. The result images show a local development walkthrough on November 5, 2026 (Asia/Dhaka; audit records are UTC, November 4 19:33 to 19:38), using the actual application and real Meta model calls. They were **recaptured after the Khata UI restyle**, replacing images from an earlier November 4 walkthrough on the previous interface. The five `parcel-*` gallery scenes other than `parcel-check.jpg` and `parcel-input.png` are AI-generated illustrations of fictional orders: they are not model inputs or app results. None are customer conversations, real parcels, pilot observations or an accuracy evaluation. This is not an accuracy evaluation, no speech recognition was tested, and no shipment was real.

## Sources and capture

All walkthrough inputs are constructed development fixtures generated by `apps/api/pocketdokani/demo/fixtures.py` and registered in `evals/split_manifest.json` before any model call (manifest SHA-256 `5bf15c596943e9affac9f27db8602a737fce6cde22aeaeddca2aff5eef6f08ae`):

- Sample conversation: two constructed chat screenshots. `conversation-input.png` is the first screenshot, byte-for-byte identical to the source stored for extraction (same SHA-256 as the earlier capture).
- Sample parcel photos for the newly confirmed order `PD-3DB0A2FA1388423A`: the `wrong-cod` and `correct` scenes, both generated by the app. `parcel-input.png` is the wrong-COD image exactly as uploaded; it replaces the earlier file only because its label carries the new order reference (the bags and the `1180/-` price are unchanged). The unreadable variant was not run.
- The sample Brown colour was corrected in the fixtures, and the generated parcel input for orders with brown bags changed. This order has blue and black bags, so the images here are not affected by that change.
- The assistant question was typed into the isolated constructed shop with the answer language set to Bangla. No audio was submitted and no transcription was tested.

The local stack used a throwaway SQLite database and local object storage, a simulated courier and a finite call allowance. The audit records (`model_runs`) show **11 provider attempts: 7 Muse Spark and 4 SAM**, plus one `assistant_turn` orchestration record. By step: extraction 1 Spark; wrong-COD check 2 SAM and 2 Spark (label read, bag comparison); matching check 2 SAM and 2 Spark; assistant 2 Spark steps. Each step ran once through the app, and no result was retried or selected. No courier booking was made in this walkthrough.

Capture notes:

- Result images are browser captures of the actual app at 430 px width and device scale 2. Only the Next.js development indicator, the sticky header and tab bar, the skip link and the sticky confirm bar were hidden with CSS so they did not cover content. No pixels were edited and no separate runs were composited.
- The seller filled only the two line discounts, which the model left blank, with 0, then confirmed. Nothing else was edited.
- `parcel-check.jpg` is the top contiguous region of the fail result: stamp, notice, COD row and the start of the order number row. The remaining rows (phone, name, address and the visible bag lines) were below the crop; they passed or matched, as listed in the observations.
- `order-review.jpg` and `order-quote.jpg` were captured after confirmation from the saved draft and show the extracted values.
- `bangla-answer.jpg` is a contiguous crop of the full-page capture taken immediately after the answer returned (question, answer, records and chips). The header and tab bar were not hidden in that capture, so the crop starts below the header and ends above the tab bar. The answer was not requested again for the capture, because it is not stored after leaving the page.
- `workspace-overview.jpg` and `docs/images/product.png` come from a separate fresh sample shop session with no model calls. The tab bar was hidden for the phone capture.
- Original local records and audits remain ignored; they are not public assets.
