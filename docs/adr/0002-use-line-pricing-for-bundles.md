# Use line pricing for bundle negotiations

Every active order line requires seller-confirmed quantity, unit price and line discount before order approval. When a conversation establishes only a bundle amount or overall discount, the seller allocates it across the lines and the allocation remains explicitly seller-confirmed, rather than evidence of customer agreement on each line. This adds a review step but keeps one money calculation and avoids a second authoritative bundle-total pricing mode during the hackathon; delivery and verified payments apply once to the whole order.

Adding or removing an item, or changing its SKU, variant or quantity, requires reconfirming the entire current line-price and discount summary before order reapproval. Prior amounts and evidence remain available, but discounts and bundle allocations never scale or redistribute silently. This conservative review step avoids tracking hidden pricing dependencies between lines; address-only changes still require order reapproval without resetting unchanged pricing confirmation.
